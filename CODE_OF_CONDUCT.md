# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and keep discussion
focused on evidence. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in any
project space, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

Results in Sigil sometimes show that someone's capability does not help or is unsafe. Discuss those
results as data. Authors deserve a fair method and a right to reply, and reviewers deserve to report
honestly without pressure.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the lead maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/dgesteves) or the contact listed on the maintainer's profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
